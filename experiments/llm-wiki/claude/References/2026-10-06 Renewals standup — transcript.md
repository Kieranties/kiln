## Gist

[[Sources/2026-10-06 Renewals standup transcript|The transcript]] of the [[2026-10-06 Renewals standup]], recorded and transcribed automatically, so speaker labels may be wrong. [[Priya Raman]] said reminder emails went out to [[Vale Rovers]] "on Friday", with a handful bounced, and that the rollover fix was up as a PR and should merge that day. [[Tom Ellis]] said the club has about 4,200 season ticket holders and the window opens on 1 November. Priya restated the retry decision: daily for three days, then email the supporter. [[Sam Okafor]] said the grace period setting is in review: per club, default seven days after the last retry, up to thirty. Tom said the club would want fourteen. Priya is to check what is live and confirm how the grace period and retries line up.

The transcript dates the meeting "Monday 6 October 2026", but 2026-10-06 is a Tuesday, so "Friday" is ambiguous.

## Why it matters

It gives the grace period's default and limit, and Vale Rovers' wish for fourteen days, which shape [[Add a grace period setting]]. Its "Friday" disagrees with the 2026-10-01 send date on [[Send renewal reminder emails]], and its 1 November window start disagrees with notes that describe the window as open in early October.
