## Gist

[[Sources/Vale Rovers onboarding notes|The onboarding notes]] say [[Vale Rovers]] signed in January 2026 and went live on 10 February 2026, the third client club after Kingsport FC and Harbour Athletic. They use the [[Club Console]] and the [[Fan App]] (white-labelled "Rovers Live"), and their payments run through [[PayRail]] on the platform account. The club has about 4,200 season ticket holders, and its renewal window runs from 1 November to 15 December. [[Tom Ellis]] is the day-to-day contact for ticketing and renewals; Mira Chen handles payouts and refunds.

## Why it matters

It explains several things the renewals project deals with: phone renewals, where club staff enter the card, fail more often; the club wants a longer grace period than the platform default, which is the [[Add a grace period setting]] work; and seats must not be released without a phone call first. Its renewal window dates sit awkwardly with reminder emails that went out on 2026-10-01.
