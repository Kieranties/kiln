## Overview

The newest of the three client clubs.

Vale Rovers signed in January 2026 and went live on 10 February 2026, after Kingsport FC and Harbour Athletic. They run memberships, season tickets and renewals on the [[Club Console]], and supporters buy and renew in the [[Fan App]], white-labelled as "Rovers Live". Payments run through [[PayRail]] on our platform account, with weekly payouts to the club. They have about 4,200 season ticket holders at Vale Park (capacity 9,800), and the renewal window runs from 1 November to 15 December each year ([[Vale Rovers — onboarding notes|onboarding notes]]). [[Tom Ellis]] confirmed both the holder count and the 1 November start at the 2026-10-06 standup ([[2026-10-06 Renewals standup — transcript|standup transcript]]).

## Working with them

Weekly renewals standup during the renewal window.

- [[Tom Ellis]], Head of Ticketing, is the day-to-day contact for ticketing and renewals and prefers a weekly call during the renewal window. Mira Chen, Finance lead, handles payouts and refunds only ([[Vale Rovers — onboarding notes|onboarding notes]]).
- Club staff log in to the Club Console through the club's own identity provider ([[Vale Rovers — onboarding notes|onboarding notes]]).
- Many older supporters renew by phone, with a club staff member entering the card, and card failures are more common for these ([[Vale Rovers — onboarding notes|onboarding notes]]).
- The club asked for a longer grace period than the platform default, agreed in principle; see [[Add a grace period setting]]. They do not want supporters' seats released without a phone call first ([[Vale Rovers — onboarding notes|onboarding notes]]).
- Tom Ellis said the club would want a fourteen-day grace period, because older supporters pay by card over the phone and it takes time ([[2026-10-06 Renewals standup — transcript|standup transcript]]).
