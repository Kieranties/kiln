## Overview

Clubs manage members, season tickets and renewals here.

## Facts

- Renewals run as a nightly batch per club.
- The nightly renewal batch recalculates the dates of open renewals, so a date fix corrects them the night after it ships ([[northwind-arena-club-console 88 Renewal date rolls over a year early|issue 88]]).
